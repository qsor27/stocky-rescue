Stocky Rescue

Shopify shut Stocky down on August 31, 2026 — but you have read-only access to export your data for at least 90 days. This free tool turns those exports into a clean, documented dataset: your purchase-order history, stocktakes, cost history — plus your reconstructed supplier list and a labeled cost report, which Stocky itself cannot export.

🔒 Your files never leave your computer — everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, ever. Anonymous page-view analytics only (self-hosted Umami) — your files and data are never sent anywhere. Source code on GitHub.

Drop your Stocky export CSVs here (or click to choose files)

Accepted: Stocky purchase-order export, stocktake report, historical-cost report, and optionally a Shopify products export (adds on-hand valuation).
